Key takeaways from experiments:
1. Task-specific fine-tuning on VQA-RAD training split boosts Closed Accuracy from 75.00% to 84.93% (+9.93%) and Open Recall from 39.34% to 45.60% (+6.26%).
2. Diffusion sampling steps Z directly control visual instruction grounding: Z=64 strikes an optimal balance between quality and inference latency.
3. Low sampling steps (Z=32) show noticeable token repetition ('the the the') and error persistence, confirming Paper Section 6.4 analysis.
